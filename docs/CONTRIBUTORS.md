# SAST Contributor One-Pager

## Why

We want to revive the baseline experience we all loved. This is a love letter to the community. The goal is to feel that fantasy again together, and to show that there is demand for an Anthem-style game. Many of the community is building demo separately, this is also an effor to align and compound our efforts.

## What

A small, replayable co-op demo you can play with friends.

**Core loop:** Build > Fly > Fight > Loot

MVP is co-op, playable, and fun. Not a live-service launch. Not an official Anthem reboot. If players do not want it, we do not expand into full production.

## Who

- Producer / Product Owner: project management, QA, scope, sprint cadence, release communication
- Technical Director: Approves code prior to merging to master
- Contributors: open to volunteers across all skillsets who claim backlog tickets
  - Programmers
  - Designers
  - Artists

## Tech stack

- Unity: game development
- GitHub: https://github.com/MrJDR/SAST. Where our production is shared.
- Discord: Coming soon. Used for frictionless communication.

## When

One sprint at a time (1 to 2 weeks). Each sprint has one goal tied to the core loop. Sprint end has a public artifact: playable build, capture, or written status. No empty promises or fake full-game launch dates.

## How

1. Join Discord when the link is posted
2. Install the pinned Unity version listed in the repo
3. Read the current sprint goal on GitHub
4. Claim one backlog ticket with acceptance criteria
5. Complete the ticket against those criteria
6. Open a pull request
7. Technical Director approves before merge to master

## Production rules

- Producer / Product Owner sets sprint priority and scope
- Technical Director owns merge approval to master
- Contributors own the craft inside the ticket they claimed
- Vision and core loop are locked; new feature ideas go to the backlog
- Design is not a democracy; discussion is welcome, scope calls sit with Producer / Product Owner
- Definition of Ready: a new contributor can execute the ticket from the text alone
- Definition of Done: acceptance criteria met and included in a reviewed PR or build
- Late-sprint feature freeze except blockers
- Contributor turnout may change every sprint; tickets are written for that
- Camera, flight, combat feedback, and loot feedback stay protected
- Custom missions and forks are allowed; main-demo mission packs wait until Fly and Fight are stable
