# SAST Vision

## One sentence

S.A.S.T. is an Anthem-inspired co-op vertical slice: a small replayable demo.

## Why

We want to revive the experience the community loved. This project is a love letter to that fantasy and a public way to show the demand is still real. If players do not want it, we do not expand into full production. There are many who are building their own demos in parallel. This is also an attempt to compound our efforts.

## Core loop

Build > Fly > Fight > Loot

## Product goal

Ship a playable multiplayer vertical slice that validates demand for this fantasy. Not a live-service launch. Not an official Anthem reboot. MVP is coop, playable, and fun.

## Team model

- Producer / Product Owner: project management, QA, scope, sprint cadence, release communication
- Technical Director: Approves code prior to merging to master
- Contributors: open to volunteers across all skillsets who claim backlog tickets
  - Programmers
  - Designers
  - Artists
- No employment or equity is implied by contributing

## Stack

- Unity (game)
- GitHub (repo, backlog, sprint board, docs, releases)
- Discord (real-time community chat)

## This is

- A community love letter to flight, combat, co-op, and loot fantasy
- A small replayable co-op demo
- An open project with a finish culture
- Demand validation for this fantasy
- Original work inspired by what players loved

## This is not

- An official Anthem reboot, sequel, remaster, or EA/BioWare project
- Use of Anthem names, logos, or assets as product identity
- A live-service promise
- A hiring pitch or studio equity scheme
- Design by committee for every missing feature from the old game
- A guarantee that EA will revive Anthem

## Success

Players can experience something close to Anthem as much as they like. That demand signal decides whether we continue past the vertical slice.
